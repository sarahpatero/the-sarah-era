This one is for you, 16-year old Sarah! Reconnected with an artist that was irrevocably important to me at such a critical period of my life. All when I was 16. First heard of him from La Mejor de Todas in May 2022, then listened to him all summer with their absolute BANGERS of Vete a la Fregada and Hasta la Muerte. Pa escuchar en la tercera noche de campa banda. Recuerdo todo.


The day of.


The concert was two days after I moved into Ann Arbor. Bought the tickets the day before the concert. Was feeling so low after move-in, riddled with overwhelm. Dorm was small, wasn't ready for senior year, felt so solemn and lonely in Tyler 3630. But getting ready for the Eslabon Armado concert lit a new fire under my ass. I did myself up so beautifully in my Urban Outfitters blue off-the-shoulder shirt. Lashes to the sky. Walked through Ann Arbor like the brand new fucking woman that I was after this summer.


Took the D2A2 bus. Concert delayed two hours. Well well well, time to wine and dine myself to a sushi dinner at Soraya! The confidence, the air of luxury that moved within me. I felt divine eating at Soraya. Absorbed the energy of a high-end hotel while I was at it too. I am so blessed.


The concert line stretched all sides of the block. Waited patiently.


The concert.


The concert itself. Oh my fucking gosh. I need to see more live music. I listen to Eslabon Armado through my headphones all the time, but only once have I FELT Eslabon Armado vibrate my entire body, pulse through my veins. I want to relive this feeling. The way I screamed when they played my favorite songs. I'm so happy I captured it all on video. I'm not one to ever forget my life. I desperately cling to all the life I can through photo and video. I've rewatched them several times since. I was a happy fucking girl. I did it all for you, 16-year old Sarah! I DID DID IT ALL FOR YOU. BECAUSE I FUCKING LOVE YOU.


Don't let me forget sitting on the balcony and how grateful I was to have upgraded there. Got my own seat where I could see the entire band and venue. Could sit down, had plenty of room to stand, dance, gritar con todo mi corazon. What an amazing concert experience. One to forever, siempre recordar.


After.


The concert ended, recorded my last few videos in the venue trying to recapitulate on how incredible of an experience it was, and searched outside pa el amor de mi vida. Nunca podía olvidar la mirada en su cara cuando nos vimos. Nos abrazamos con dulzura. Me sentí muy bien cuidada. Caminamos a su camión, la sonrisa en su cara cuando abrí la puerta. Me sorprendió con flores y chocolates. El sentimiento perdura en mi. Llévame de vuelta!!!!! La mejor noche de mi puta vida.
