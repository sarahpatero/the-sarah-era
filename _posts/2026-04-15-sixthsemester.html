It certainly hasn’t hit me yet, but my goodness, it has been such an amazing semester. This entire year in fact has been the best year of my college career. I’ve seen myself grow to such new heights I would’ve never imagined. I finally built some momentum in terms of where I want to be when I grow up.


But let me talk more specifically about this semester now that I’ve passed FM!!! Back in December, I imagined what this semester would be like. I have to take Exam P in January, have an entire winterguard season, then take Exam FM in April. Going from no exams passed to being two for two in just three months. I feel so satisfied with myself now that I can see it all coming together.


I’m also doing incredibly well in terms of taking care of myself. I started working out like a maniac again, and the only thing I’m upset about is why I didn’t start sooner. The Group-X pass has provided me so much more than just fun workout classes to go to. I genuinely feel like I’ve found myself again through fitness. I look in the mirror and absolutely love what I see again. And hello…I just interviewed to be a Group-X instructor for Barre today??? So surreal to see how much life can change in just one month.


And this summer is bound to be great. I don’t have an internship, but it is genuinely for the best. This could be my last summer of freedom. Ever. I have my entire life to work. But this summer, I can set my own schedule with how I want to study for MAS-1, be a waitress, and take care of my health and fitness. I can relax and enjoy doing the things I love. I can go on long bike rides to Spencer park, I can spin flags outside whenever I want. I can go to Powerhouse Gym with Mag. I can go to Birmingham and eat a chocolate croissant from Paris Baguette. I’m seeing it as the entire world is my oyster for this summer. I’m going to let loose and let myself live and be free.


I can feel a quiet confidence settling into myself and where I will be in the future. I feel safe in the fact that I will have a stable job and income after leaving college. This was my biggest qualm for my first two years of college; where will I be, what will I even be doing after I graduate? I can now relax and feel safe knowing that everything is certainly going to work out for me.
