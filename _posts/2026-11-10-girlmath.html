Whenever I tell people I'm majoring in math, they automatically assume I'm a genius. But I graduated high school thinking to myself, "Finally, never have to take another math class again."


My math major started as an experiment to see if I could actually do it. Or in other words, let me just major in math for fun. Let me see if I could hold it down when the going gets tough.


Now I will graduate with my dream job at my dream company in my dream city. All because I treated majoring in math as something I should just try. What's the harm in trying?


Math has brought out the best in me. Majoring in math has been the most fulfilling experience of my college career. Not because I’m a gifted genius, but precisely because I’m not.


I learned how to push through. I watched myself persevere through struggle. I failed classes, never earned an A, withdrew, and doubted myself many times. And along the way, I collected so much evidence that I am a disciplined, persistent person who can do anything I set my mind to.


It’s been incredible to watch myself grow.


And so many things in my life start with the same question: "Wouldn't it be fun if I…?"


  • Wouldn't it be fun if I tried out colorguard instead of alto sax? Now I've been in a collegiate colorguard for four years.
  • Wouldn't it be fun if I became an exercise instructor? Now I teach barre at my college.
  • Wouldn't it be fun if I joined Bengali club even though I'm not Bengali? Now I have so many amazing friends and memories.
  • Wouldn't it be fun if I hosted a party for my friends to all meet each other? Now all my friends are friends with each other!
  • Wouldn't it be fun if I started a blog? Now I know how to code in HTML and CSS, and I have a neat portfolio of my own writing.

That's my kind of girl math: focus on having fun first and foremost, and good things will come.


Too often, we put so much seriousness into planning our future that we forget life is genuinely a game. Living life like it's a game has filled my days with much more joy and spontaneity. Do things purely for fun, try everything, and don't be afraid to fail.


Especially if you're an anxious person, do everything for fun. Go to a job interview for fun. Start a business for fun. Start a blog for fun. Talk to that stranger for fun. Anxiety feeds on importance. Don't make everything a matter of life and death.


Every time I feel scared, I remind myself that almost every good thing in my life came after I decided to be brave. It's a lot easier to be brave when you frame it as "I'm just doing this for fun."
