Isn't it ironic? I tell people I'm majoring in math and they automatically assume I'm a genius. But I graduated high school thinking to myself, "Finally, never have to take another math class again."


Math major started as an experiment to see if I could fr do it. To see if I could hold it down when the going gets tough.


Now I will graduate with my dream job at my dream company in my dream city. All because I treated majoring in math as something I should try just to see if I could make it.


And so many things in my life start with the same question: "Wouldn't it be fun if I…?"


  • Wouldn't it be fun if I tried out colorguard instead of alto sax? Now I've been in a collegiate colorguard for four years.
  • Wouldn't it be fun if I became an exercise instructor? Now I teach barre at my college.
  • Wouldn't it be fun if I joined Bengali club even though I'm not Bengali? Now I have so many amazing friends and memories.
  • Wouldn't it be fun if I hosted a party for my friends to all meet each other? Now all my friends are friends with each other!
  • Wouldn't it be fun if I started a blog? Now I know how to code in HTML and CSS, and I have a neat portfolio of my own writing.

That's my kind of girl math: focus on having fun first and foremost, and good things will come.


Too often, we put so much seriousness into planning our future that we forget life is genuinely a game. You can play the game of life, like seriously. Do things for fun, try everything, and don't be afraid to fail.


If you're an anxious person, do everything for fun. Go to a job interview for fun. Submit documents for fun. Start a blog for fun. Anxiety feeds on importance. Don't make everything a matter of life and death.


Every time I feel scared, I remind myself that almost every good thing in my life came after I decided to be brave.
