Ask yourself this question: how does someone who has a fun and interesting life intentionally live?


What comes to mind first is that they are ruthless about caring for their health. They cut out the baggage, and the things and people that don't belong.


Layer one: vitality.


This is the bare minimum. You need vitality that can be felt from miles away. The energy needed for a full life is no joke, so build the routine that gives you structure, and that structure decides when you get to be spontaneous.


It's not good to live a completely new life every single day. You need something to anchor you: waking up at the same time every day, eating the same foods, tea time, an exercise class, a daily walk. Too much spontaneity is draining. And nothing will drain you more than someone in your life who doesn't belong.


Spontaneity isn't completely spontaneous. It's quite intentional at the core. You won't feel energized to put yourself out there if you don't look good, and if you don't look good, you won't feel good. Why would I want to talk to a stranger if I look and feel like a bum?


Once you learn how to cultivate vigorous vitality, then we can talk.


Layer two: spontaneity with yourself.


After you've mastered the art of unending energy, you're ready for the next layer of intentional spontaneity: spontaneity with yourself.


You need to make sure you can go with the flow before you bring someone else into your world. Build your confidence and learn how to deeply enjoy your own company. This is important. How can you enjoy time with someone else before you enjoy your own? Be careful when a naked person offers you a shirt.


So practice spontaneity on your own. Get out of autopilot. Eat somewhere new. Take a different route to class. Wear the outfit you've always wanted to wear, now.


Layer three: bringing other people into your world.


Start with intentionality. Get in the habit of seeing your friends regularly. At least every other day, you should be seeing a friend or meeting someone new. Either you deepen a relationship with someone you already know, or someone completely introduces you to a new world.


See every person as a one-in-a-billion bond that can never exist again in the entirety of the universe. Each person is completely unique. They will show you an entirely different side of life, if you let them. If you're open to it.


It's more than spontaneity.


This has turned into a talk about spontaneity. Spontaneity goes hand in hand with fun and interesting, but fun and interesting is so much more than that. It's about who you are in full expression: out loud, unapologetically. It's spending quality time alone, fulfilling yourself. Wholehearted self-expression and uniqueness are as interesting as it gets.


Unapologetic self-expression, spontaneity, and amazing people in your life.


Do things that scare you a little bit. Stretch your rubber band. Step out of your comfort zone. Say yes anyway. Chase your genuine curiosity instead of the safe path. Seek truth and true connection. Recognize the inherent beauty in every person you meet.
